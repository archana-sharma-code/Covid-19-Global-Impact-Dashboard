## 📊 Key Insights Summary

1. **Deaths per Reported Case Fell Sharply:** Deaths per 1,000 reported cases fell from 24.5 to 2.5 between early 2021 peak and Omicron peak (2022), consistent with vaccination, prior infection, and milder variant

2. **Reporting Artifacts:** Highest 7-day case value (6.3M/day, Dec 2022) is not a global surge - China accounts for 86% of cases in that window

3. **CFR Variation:** Ranged from 0.05% (Brunei) to 4.88% (Peru) among 129 countries with >100K cases

4. **No Detectable Vaccination Speed-Death Correlation:** Spearman correlation between days to 50% vaccination and deaths per million = -0.17 (p = 0.20); +0.07 after controlling for median age (p = 0.62)

5. **Rollout Speed:** 87 countries reached 50% fully vaccinated, from 97 days (Israel) to 766 (Sierra Leone); median 257 days

6. **Regional Distribution:** Deaths per million: South America 3,104, Europe 2,815, North America 2,784, Oceania 731, Asia 347, Africa 182

7. **Early Spread:** Median doubling time of cumulative cases was 9.6 days in March-April 2020
